## Cross-cutting techniques

- **Risset** — spectral mutation, endless glissandi, bell functions (Dodge book lineage)
- **Cook** — classic Csound pedagogy (buzz, chant, fof choir, gbuzz bass)
- **Comajuncosas / DrB_Favorites** — analog FM pads, TB-303, karplus, iterated sine
- **Varo** — Yamaha-style GM instruments (marimba, strings, brass, pluck bass)
- **Smaragdis** — granular / FM hybrids (anagrain, altfm)
- **Mikelson** — additive + analog bass/drum workshop models
